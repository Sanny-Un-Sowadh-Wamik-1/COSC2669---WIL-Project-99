## Overview

Pests, infestations, mould and damp can harm people's health and damage property if not dealt with quickly. In extreme cases they are an urgent repair. Pests and infestations might include mice, rats, possums, fleas, ants or termites. Renters who see signs of pests, infestations, mould or rising damp should tell the rental provider immediately.

## Who is responsible

The law doesn't always say who is responsible; it can depend on who contributed to the problem. If the issue comes from the building structure, the renter can request an urgent repair. The renter may be responsible if their actions contributed, for example not using the bathroom ventilation fan leading to mould, not removing rubbish that attracted ants, mice or cockroaches, or not treating their pet for fleas. The rental provider may be responsible if a problem with the property caused mould or damp or let pests in, for example broken window frames letting rain in, pests present when the renter moved in, or holes in the roof. If there is disagreement about who pays, either party can apply to VCAT.

## When pests or mould are an urgent repair

If pests or mould caused by or related to the building structure make the property unsafe or unsecure to live in, they are an urgent repair. Renters may need to provide proof the mould is dangerous, such as an assessment report by an occupational hygienist, environmental health professional or council, or a doctor's report for people with existing health conditions such as asthma. Renters may apply to VCAT to recover the cost of health assessments or medical reports.
