## Rental providers must fit smoke alarms

Smoke alarms are compulsory in all Victorian residential properties, and rental providers must ensure their property is fitted with them. Buildings constructed after 1 August 1997 must have hard-wired smoke alarms with battery back-up; older buildings can have battery-powered smoke alarms. Smoke alarms must meet Australian Standard AS 3786. Rooming houses must have hard-wired smoke alarms.

## Rental providers must make sure smoke alarms are working

Rental providers must ensure all smoke alarms are correctly installed and working, are fitted with batteries or replacement batteries, and are tested at least once every 12 months according to the manufacturer's instructions. On or before the start of a rental agreement, the rental provider must give the renter written information about how each smoke alarm operates, how to test it, and the renter's obligation not to tamper with smoke alarms and to report any that are not working.

## Renters and smoke alarms

Renters must tell the rental provider if a smoke alarm is faulty or not working. Renters must not deactivate, remove or interfere with a smoke alarm; doing so is a breach of duty and the rental provider may give a Notice of breach of duty. When renters move out, they should leave the batteries in the smoke alarm.

## Broken smoke alarms are urgent repairs

If told that a smoke alarm is not working, rental providers must immediately arrange for it to be repaired or replaced as an urgent repair. If the rental provider cannot be contacted or does not fix it immediately, the renter can authorise and pay for an urgent repair of up to $2,500, and the rental provider must pay them back within 7 days. A hard-wired smoke alarm must be installed by a qualified electrician, but anyone can change a smoke alarm battery.

## Water tanks in bushfire prone areas

For agreements entered into after 29 March 2021, if a property is in a bushfire prone area and a water tank is required for firefighting, the rental provider must ensure the tank is maintained in good repair and is clean and full at the start of the agreement.
