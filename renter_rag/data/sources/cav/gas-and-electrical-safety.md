## Urgent gas and electrical repairs

Gas leaks, dangerous electrical faults, a failure of a supplied appliance for hot water, water, cooking, heating or laundry, and a failure of the gas, electricity or water supply are urgent repairs. If the rental provider cannot be reached or does not respond immediately, renters may arrange repairs up to $2,500 and be reimbursed within 7 days. Symptoms of carbon monoxide poisoning include tiredness, shortness of breath, headaches, nausea, vomiting and dizziness.

## Gas safety checks

For rental agreements entered into after 29 March 2021, rental providers must have a gas safety check done every 2 years by a licensed or registered gasfitter (endorsed for Type A Gas Appliances Servicing). If no check has been done in the past 2 years when a renter moves in, one must be done as soon as possible. Rental providers must tell renters the date of the last gas safety check and any outstanding recommendations before the agreement is entered into.

## Electrical safety checks

For rental agreements entered into after 29 March 2021 (or fixed terms that become periodic after that date), all electrical installations and fittings must be checked by a licensed electrician at least once every 2 years, in accordance with AS/NZS 3019. From 29 March 2023, all power outlets and lighting circuits must connect to a switchboard-type circuit breaker and a switchboard-type residual current device. Rental providers must tell renters the date of the last electrical safety check before the agreement is entered into.

## Records of safety checks

Rental providers must keep records of the most recent gas and electrical safety checks until the next check, and must give the renter a copy within 7 days of a written request.
