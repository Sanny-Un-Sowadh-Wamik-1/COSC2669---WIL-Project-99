## Methods of payment

A rental agreement must specify the rent amount and payment frequency. Rent must be paid to the rental provider according to the terms of the agreement. The renter and rental provider should agree on payment methods before the agreement starts. The rental provider must provide at least one fee-free payment method (other than the renter's own bank fees), must allow payment by Commonwealth Centrepay and by electronic funds transfer, must disclose any fees associated with the chosen payment method, and cannot require post-dated cheques. Neither party can require reimbursement for account set-up or payment processing costs.

## Illegal fees and charges

Rental providers cannot charge fees for issuing payment cards, setting up or using direct debit, processing payments, or late payment of rent. Third-party platforms used to manage rental agreements cannot charge renters fees (standard bank fees excluded).

## Payment frequency and timing

Rental agreements specify the payment schedule: weekly, fortnightly, calendar monthly, or another agreed time. Renters paying by electronic transfer should allow 24 hours so the payment is not treated as late.

## Charging rent in advance

Rental providers cannot ask for more than one month's rent in advance. Exceptions: if rent is paid weekly, the maximum rent in advance is 14 days; if the rent is more than $900 a week, there is no limit. Rooming houses, caravan parks and Part 4A sites have additional restrictions capping rent in advance at between 14 and 28 days depending on the type of property.

## Stopping rent payments

Renters must not stop paying rent until they agree with their rental provider on the date payments stop. The obligation to pay rent continues even if there are problems with repairs, the lease is ending, or a notice to vacate has been given.

## Providing rent receipts

A receipt must be provided immediately if rent is paid in person, or within five business days if the renter asks for one. A receipt must include the rental provider's name and signature, the property address, the date of payment and the period the payment covers, the amount paid, and clearly state that it is a receipt. Records of rent payments must be kept for at least 12 months.
