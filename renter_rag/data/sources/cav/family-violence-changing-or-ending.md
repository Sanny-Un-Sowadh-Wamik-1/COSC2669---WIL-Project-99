## Changing or ending your rental agreement

If you are experiencing family violence and need to change your rental agreement to keep yourself or your children safe, you can apply to VCAT, whether or not your name is on the current agreement. Parents or guardians can also apply on behalf of children. You can ask VCAT to order the rental provider to create a new rental agreement in your name that excludes the person using violence, or to end the agreement early without penalty.

## Evidence VCAT considers

VCAT considers evidence such as family violence safety notices or intervention orders, personal safety intervention orders, financial records, photos, recordings or messages, and written statements from family, friends, support workers, health professionals, police, religious leaders, crisis accommodation providers, child protection staff, employers or lawyers. VCAT also weighs the risk to your or your children's safety and whether the person using violence has been arrested or has bail conditions. VCAT must hear these applications within three business days.

## After VCAT changes or ends the agreement

The rental provider may request a new condition report or inspection with proper notice. VCAT may decide how the bond is divided or remove the person using violence from the bond. An excluded person cannot enter the property but keeps some rights, such as being represented at inspections and applying to VCAT to end the agreement early.
