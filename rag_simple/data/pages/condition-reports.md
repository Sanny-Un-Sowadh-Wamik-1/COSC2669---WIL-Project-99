## What is a condition report?

A condition report records the state of a rental property at the start of a rental agreement, including any issues with cleanliness, damage or things that don't work, covering fixtures, fittings and interior and exterior features. All rental properties, rooming houses, caravans, caravan sites and Part 4A park sites must have condition reports. The rental provider or their agent prepares and signs the report and gives the renter two paper copies (or one electronic copy) before the renter moves in. Condition reports are important evidence in disputes about bond claims, cleaning or who is responsible for damage.

## Condition reports at the start of the agreement

Rental providers must give the renter a signed condition report, using the official template, before the renter moves in. Renters should check it is accurate, add comments if needed, and return a signed copy within five business days. If no condition report is provided before the renter moves in, the renter may prepare their own and give it to the rental provider within the same timeframe. Taking dated, labelled photographs of the property at the start, especially of any existing damage, is recommended. Both parties should keep copies throughout the rental agreement.

## Disputes about the condition report

Either party may apply to VCAT within 30 days of the start of the rental agreement if they believe the condition report is inaccurate or incomplete.

## Condition reports at the end of the agreement

The rental provider or agent must complete the end-of-agreement section of the condition report within 10 days of the rental agreement ending, with the renter present or given a reasonable opportunity to attend. Rental providers may claim bond for new damage (beyond fair wear and tear), needed repairs, additional cleaning or missing items, supported by the condition report.

## Cleaning responsibilities

Renters must keep the property in a reasonably clean condition. When they leave, the property must be returned reasonably clean and in a similar condition to when they moved in, allowing for fair wear and tear. For agreements signed from 29 March 2021, rental providers cannot require professional cleaning at the end of the agreement unless professional cleaning is needed to return the property to the condition it was in at the start (allowing for fair wear and tear).
