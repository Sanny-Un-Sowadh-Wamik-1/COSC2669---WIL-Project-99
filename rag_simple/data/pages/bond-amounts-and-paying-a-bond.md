## What is a bond?

A bond is a payment that rental providers (landlords) can request from renters at the start of a rental agreement (lease). It is security for the rental provider and covers potential costs when the renter moves out. The Residential Tenancies Bond Authority (RTBA) holds bonds in trust. The bond is the renter's money and should be repaid to the renter in most circumstances. Rent and bond are different: a bond cannot be used to pay rent. Renters who have paid a bond receive a condition report before moving in. Eligible renters may transfer existing bond money to a new property through the Portable Rental Bond Scheme.

## Maximum bond amounts

Generally, a bond cannot be more than one month's rent. A higher bond is only allowed if the weekly rent is more than $900, or if VCAT makes an order because of the character of the property or the quality of its furnishings. Rooming houses: the maximum bond is 28 days' rent for fixed-term agreements or 14 days' rent otherwise. Caravan parks: the bond cannot be more than one month's rent. Residential park site tenants: the maximum bond is one month's rent (where rent is $900 a week or less).

## How and when bonds are paid

Paying a bond is not compulsory unless the rental agreement requires it. Renters may pay the rental provider, who then lodges it with the RTBA (captured text: within 14 days), pay the RTBA directly, or use the Portable Rental Bond Scheme. The RTBA issues a receipt with a bond number within 7 days of receiving the money. When several renters pay the RTBA directly, only one renter can submit the full payment, so co-renters should decide beforehand who pays and how they reimburse each other.

## Homes Victoria bond loans

Government bond loans help people on low incomes who cannot pay a bond any other way.

## Extra bond requests

Rental providers can only ask for additional bond in these circumstances: long-term agreements (more than 5 years, renewed for at least another 5 years), or when the renter makes certain modifications that the rental provider will have to reverse. Renters can challenge extra bond requests at VCAT, which considers whether paying would cause severe hardship.
