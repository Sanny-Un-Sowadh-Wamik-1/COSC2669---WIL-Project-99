## Reasons for claiming bond

The rental provider might claim the bond as compensation if the renter damaged the property, owes rent or charges, has not left the property reasonably clean, is missing items (for example, locks), or made certain changes to the property without asking. Rental providers cannot claim bond for fair wear and tear or for damage the rental provider was responsible for repairing.

## Final inspections

A rental provider, property manager or agent will do a final inspection at the end of the rental agreement. The condition report should be used to compare any changes to the property during the agreement. The renter must be present during the final inspection or have been given a reasonable opportunity to be there.

## Fair wear and tear

Fair wear and tear is deterioration because of reasonable use of the property or natural environmental forces. Faded curtains or worn kitchen bench tops are fair wear and tear, whereas ripped curtains or broken bench tops might be considered damage.

## Bond disputes

When renters and rental providers disagree about a bond claim, any party can apply to VCAT or Rental Dispute Resolution Victoria (RDRV), who will hear the reasoning of each party and make the decision for them.

## How to claim a bond with the RTBA

Bonds are claimed using the RTBA website. Rental providers must start a claim within 14 days of the rental agreement ending, unless a renter starts a claim first. When a claim is submitted, every renter on the bond receives an email with a link to review and accept the claim. Renters must accept within 14 days or the claim expires.

## Claims with a VCAT or RDRV order

An order from VCAT or RDRV can be submitted to the RTBA, which will distribute the bond in accordance with the order within one business day after validating it.

## How the RTBA repays a bond

The RTBA repays bonds to nominated bank accounts, including international accounts. Account details must be accurate because the RTBA doesn't keep renters' banking details on record and can't double check them.
