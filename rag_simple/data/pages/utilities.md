## Water and sewerage

Renters in properties with individual water meters must pay for water usage and sewage disposal, unless the rental provider agrees otherwise in the rental agreement. Rental providers remain responsible for infrastructure (supply) charges and for maintaining water tanks, unless the renter caused the damage.

## Electricity, gas and oil

Rental providers must pay for installing and connecting electricity, gas or oil supply. Where the supply is metered, renters pay for usage unless the rental agreement says otherwise. Renters pay for bottled gas.

## Phone and internet

Renters must pay for the ongoing usage costs for phone and internet, unless the rental provider agrees otherwise. Rental providers pay for the initial installation of a connection, but renters may be charged reconnection fees for an existing line.

## Television

Television is not an essential service. Renters may ask for an antenna to be installed but rental providers don't have to do it.

## Excessive usage charges

Rental providers pay for bills that exceed normal usage only when the renter reported the problem promptly, the renter didn't cause the fault, and the service provider is not responsible.

## Appliance efficiency and moving out

Rental providers must replace appliances that do not meet efficiency requirements and pay the extra utility costs until they are replaced. When renters move out they must tell utility companies the date they are leaving; rental providers pay the costs between the renter leaving and the next renter moving in.
