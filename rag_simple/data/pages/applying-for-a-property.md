## Pricing rules for advertised rental properties

Properties must be advertised at a fixed rent. Rental providers and agents cannot advertise a property with a price range, ask for or accept rent higher than the advertised amount, seek more than one month's rent in advance, advertise a property that does not meet the rental minimum standards, or charge fees to view a property. Concerns about advertised properties can be reported to Consumer Affairs Victoria.

## Rental application form

From 31 March 2026, rental providers must use the prescribed rental application form, which limits the information collected to what is needed to assess suitability and capacity to pay rent.

## Information that can be requested

Personal details (full name, date of birth, current address, phone, email); employment details (current or most recent employer, net weekly income, date employment ended); up to two financial documents from pay slips, a bank statement without transaction details, or Centrelink payment statements (or, for irregular income, employment history or proof of savings); up to two identity documents (such as a driver licence, Medicare card, passport or student ID); rental history (contact details of previous rental providers); personal references; the desired lease term and move-in date. Rental providers using tenancy databases must tell applicants which databases they use.

## Information that cannot be requested

Rental providers and agents cannot ask about history of legal action or disputes with rental providers, bond claim history, bank statements showing daily transactions (applicants can redact them), protected attributes under the Equal Opportunity Act 2010 without written justification, or anything outside the prescribed application form.

## Choosing a renter

Rental providers can choose applicants based on suitability, such as rental history and capacity to pay, but discrimination based on attributes such as age, disability, race or religion is unlawful. Rental providers generally cannot refuse applicants with children under 16, except where the property is the rental provider's main residence or is unsuitable for children.

## Holding deposits

A holding deposit may be requested before the agreement is signed. It must be refunded once both parties sign the agreement, or refunded if no agreement is reached.
